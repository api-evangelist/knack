FormDr (also searched as Form Doctor) is a well-regarded tool. If your practice needs to move patients from paper intake packets to digital forms, have them sign electronically before an appointment, and push completed submissions into your EHR, FormDr does that job well. This comparison is not an argument that FormDr is the wrong tool.
