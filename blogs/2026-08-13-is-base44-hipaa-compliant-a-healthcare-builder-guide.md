Is Base44 HIPAA Compliant? A Healthcare Builder Guide Base44 is not HIPAA compliant. As of August 2026, Base44 does not offer a Business Associate Agreement, and its own Terms of Service explicitly restrict protected health information from entering the platform without a separately negotiated written agreement.
