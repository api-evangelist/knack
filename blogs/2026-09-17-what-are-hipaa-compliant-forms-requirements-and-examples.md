HIPAA-compliant forms are not just digital versions of paper intake packets. They are a specific category of tool that meets federal requirements for collecting, storing, and protecting protected health information (PHI). Most healthcare organizations use forms every day without fully knowing which ones qualify as HIPAA-compliant and which ones create compliance risk.
